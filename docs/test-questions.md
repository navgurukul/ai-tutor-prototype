# Questions that work in the prototype

The prototype has no real AI model behind it. Its answers are written in advance, and it picks one by looking for certain words in the question. This page lists every question that has an answer, so you can test the chat without guessing.

There are 70 answers: 41 for Class 6 and 29 for Class 7, in Science and Mathematics.

## Before you test

- **Use a Class 6 or Class 7 profile.** The search stays inside the student’s own class, so a Class 6 profile cannot get a Class 7 answer. Profiles for Class 8 to 12 have no books yet and always get "I couldn’t find this".
- **You do not have to use the exact question.** Any sentence that contains one of the trigger words works. "hey, I want to understand how plants grow from a seed" gives the same answer as "How does a seed grow into a plant?".
- **A trigger word can be just the start of a word.** "evaporat" covers evaporate, evaporation and evaporating.
- **If a question has trigger words for two topics**, the more specific topic usually wins. "Is Pluto a planet?" gives the dwarf planets answer, not the planets answer.
- **It does not remember the chat.** Each question is matched on its own words, so follow-ups do not work. After "How do scientists find answers?", "can you explain the third step more" has no trigger word and gets "I couldn’t find this", and "what is the hypothesis" gives the whole scientific method answer again, because "hypothesis" is one of its trigger words.
- **"Make It Simpler"** works once on every answer in the tables below.

## The three results you can get

| Result | What you see | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A full answer | "Found it in Science, Chapter 12, page 215", then the answer with its source and "Make It Simpler" | Any question in the tables below |
| The chapter, but no answer | The chapter is found, then: "I can’t explain this one yet, because this is a test version of the app." | A topic from a Class 6 or 7 chapter that has no written answer. See the examples below |
| Not found | "I couldn’t find this in your Class 6 books. Try asking it another way, or ask your teacher." | Anything outside Class 6 and 7 Science and Mathematics. See the examples below |

## Hard paths, from the Scenarios panel

Open **Scenarios** in the dark bar at the top. Each switch changes what the next question does.

| Switch | Then ask | What you should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next search finds nothing | Any question from the tables | The "I couldn’t find this" message, once. The switch turns itself off |
| Next answer fails | Any question from the tables | "Something went wrong." with a "Try Again" button, once |
| Slow laptop | Any question from the tables | Every step takes 3 times longer. After 10 seconds a line appears: "Still working on it. Just a few more moments." |
| Cold model, "Load again" | Any question, straight away | "Getting ready" shows as the first step |

## What is not real yet

- **Page numbers are placeholders.** The chapter names and numbers are the real ones from the NCERT books, but the pages have not been checked against the printed books.
- **Class 7 Mathematics covers Part 1 of the book only**, which is chapters 1 to 8.
- **The answers do not come from the books’ own words.** They were written for this prototype, for an 11-year-old reader, and their facts were checked.

This page was made from `src/data/answers6.ts`, `src/data/answers7.ts` and `src/data/textbooks.ts`. If an answer is added or its trigger words change, this page must be updated too.
